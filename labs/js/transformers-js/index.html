Alert similarity, in the browser

Transformers.js embeds every alert with Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 on your device and ranks them by cosine similarity to your query. No server is involved after the model is cached.

#CosineAlert
Model: Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions, quantised ONNX, about 23 MB, Apache-2.0). Library: @xenova/transformers from jsDelivr. Server-side equivalent in the capstone: internal/embeddings with copilot embed. Vectors from this model are not comparable with vectors from nomic-embed-text or text-embedding-3-small; never mix them in one index.
